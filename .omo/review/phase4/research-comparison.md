# research-comparison: dd-iast-go vs Orchestrion research PR DataDog/orchestrion#858

Inputs: `phase1/research/{00-digest,runtime,injector,shadow,cases}.md` (R#/I#/S# checks), phase-2 reports, phase-3 verdicts (adjusted severity authoritative). HEAD 2e23b46.

**Verdict:** dd-iast-go deliberately traded the research's free alias resolution for hard bounds, request isolation, and no pinning — and won on those axes. But the research's two reproduced failure modes (stale taint on reused memory, unobserved in-place mutation) both materialized in dd-iast-go as CONFIRMED Highs, and the coarse-propagation and redaction policies are worse than the research's.

## 2. Defects where the research lesson was not handled (deduped by root cause)

All CONFIRMED in phase 3 unless noted; severity = adjusted.

- **E1 Unanchored Builder/Buffer writer view + numeric view match → stale taint and cross-request bleed after GC address reuse.** High. `iast/propagation/writer.go:202-205` (view not anchored), `internal/taint/store/writer.go:448-462` (view equality on numbers, any receiver). Ids: store-identity-gc-F1, crash-diff-bytes-F1, store-writer-F2, life-cross-request-F2, life-cross-request-F3 (B's clean constant SQL reported with A's source, 3/3 woven). This is exactly the research's spare-capacity/address-reuse lesson (`res-runtime/stale_capacity.out.txt`); research paid pinning or shadow-clearing, dd-iast-go releases the anchor without invalidating the view. Evidence: `evidence/fx-store-identity-gc-F1/`, `evidence/fx-crash-diff-bytes-F1/`. Fix: anchor the writer-view backing (retain until finish) and match views by identity, not (len,cap) equality.
- **E2 Adopted app-owned `io.ReadAll` slice reused across requests.** High. `internal/taint/request/reader.go:67-88,119`, `internal/taint/store/lookup.go:120-196`. Id: life-cross-request-F1 — A's recycled body slice refilled to the same length matches A's root key while A is still active; B reports A's source. Evidence: `evidence/fx-prop-owner-isolation-F1/` dir (fx-life-cross-request-F1). Fix: content check (e.g. compare a cheap fingerprint) before accepting a foreign active-owner key match.
- **E3 Unhooked reader Reset/reassignment keeps the original binding.** High. `iast/bufio/orchestrion.yml:15-34`, `internal/taint/request/reader.go:27-39`, `binding.go:95-108,166-203`. ## 3. Coverage: research covers what dd-iast-go does not

- **Free aliasing:** research interval overlap makes every sub-view tainted automatically; dd-iast-go exact keys mean any alias outside wrapped root-module calls is clean — regexp, dependency reslices, method values, indirect calls. Documented (res-shadow-F2, Info) — coverage, not a bug, per the digest.
- **Builtins:** research hooks `append`/`copy`/`clear` and index writes; dd-iast-go observes none (README-documented trade-off; E1–E3 show the consequence).
- **Scalars, maps/channels, method values:** research tracks byte/rune locals and `tainted` packets; dd-iast-go has no equivalent.
- **Shadow toolchain:** nearly universal propagation including coarse control flow, darwin/arm64 fork.
- **Buffer read family:** research `BufferBytes`/`BufferNext` rely on aliasing; dd-iast-go hooks writers but missed reader Reset/reassignment (E3).

## 4. What dd-iast-go does better

- **Bounds:** fixed arrays — 256×128 shards, 16,384 values, 8 MiB/2 MiB charges, 512 roots/owner, 64 ranges (`internal/taint/store/limits.go`) vs research's unbounded pinned owner maps and 65,536-entry tables that latch `unknown`. dd-iast-go drops with counters; no process-wide saturation latch was found (E8/E9 are exceedances, not absence of bounds).
- **Isolation:** one owner per sampled request with `ownerGen`/`rootGen` staleness checks vs research's process-global `StartRequest` where concurrent requests evict each other. Statically holds; bleed enters only through E1–E3 defects.
- **No pinning leak:** anchors release at owner finish (`owner.go:162-201`) vs research pinning every tainted array for process lifetime (README:76-86).
- **Cheap gate + non-blocking concurrency:** `HasValues()`→`ActiveStore()`→`MayContain` before any slow path, TryLock drops vs research's full interval scan under a blocking RWMutex.
- **Deployability:** stock toolchain, stock Orchestrion weaving vs a forked Go 1.26.1 runtime (darwin/arm64 only). The review's bench corpus (`evidence/bench/`) gives dd-iast-go measured overhead the research never produced.

**Contradiction note:** artifacts agree except `life-admission-F1` (refuted then overwritten to CONFIRMED High — reported as DISPUTED above) and perf-contention-F1 (phase-2 High, phase-3 REFUTED to Info).
